About Us

Professional graphic design and printing — built on creativity and care.

Kairos Design and Print is a creative studio based in Nigeria, founded and led by Folarin Abiodun David. We combine professional graphic design with high-quality large-format printing, so you can take an idea from a rough sketch all the way to a finished, professionally printed product — without juggling three different vendors.

From a single stack of business cards to a full brand overhaul with building wraps and banners, every job gets the same treatment: clean, considered design, honest pricing, and print quality we are proud to put our name on. We are creative, reliable, quality-focused and genuinely invested in the people we work with.
